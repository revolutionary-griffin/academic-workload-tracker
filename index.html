📖 How to Use Academic Work Tracker

🚀 Getting Started

  1. Sign In: Click "Sign in with Google" to authenticate with your Google account
  2. Grant Permissions: Allow the app to create and manage Google Sheets on your behalf
  3. Automatic Setup: The app will automatically create a new Google Sheet to store your data
  4. Start Tracking: Begin entering your daily work hours!

📅 Daily Tracking

  1. Select Date: Use the date picker or navigation buttons (◀ Day, Day ▶) to choose which day to track
  2. Choose View: Switch between Day, Week, or Month view depending on your preference
  3. Enter Hours: Input the hours spent on each task (you can use decimals like 1.5 for 1 hour 30 minutes)
  4. Auto-Save: Data automatically saves to Google Sheets after 2 seconds of inactivity
  5. Manual Save: Click "Save to Google Sheets" to save immediately

Tip: Use the "Today" button to quickly jump to today's date!

📊 Statistics

The Statistics tab helps you visualize your workload distribution:

  • Period Selection: Choose from This Week, This Month, This Quarter, This Year, or a Custom Range
  • Total Hours Chart: See total hours spent in each category
  • Category Breakdown: View a pie chart showing the percentage distribution
  • Weekly Trend: Track how your daily hours change over time
  • Summary Stats: View total hours, average per day, and category percentages

Tip: Click "Refresh" after selecting a period to update the statistics!

🎯 Workload Allocation

Set and track your target workload distribution:

  1. Set Targets: Enter your desired percentage allocation for Research, Teaching, and Service (must total 100%)
  2. View Actuals: See your actual allocation based on the last 30 days of data
  3. Compare: Use the chart to compare your target vs. actual allocation
  4. Save: Click "Save Allocation" to store your targets

⚙ Settings

Customize the tracker to fit your needs:

  • Task Categories: Add or remove tasks within each category (Research, Teaching, Service, Other)
  • Week Start Day: Choose which day your week starts (Sunday, Monday, etc.)
  • Reminders: Enable reminders to notify you when you haven't recorded work for several days
  • Google Sheet: Create a new sheet or connect to an existing one

💡 Tips & Best Practices

  • Daily Tracking: Try to record your hours daily for the most accurate data
  • Week View: Use Week view to quickly fill in multiple days at once
  • Access Your Sheet: You can open and view your Google Sheet directly in Google Sheets - the sheet ID is shown in Settings
  • Data Ownership: You own your data completely - it's stored in your personal Google Sheet
  • Backup: Your Google Sheet is automatically backed up by Google Drive
  • Offline Work: Changes are cached locally and sync when you click Save

🔒 Privacy & Security

  • Your Data Only: This app only accesses YOUR Google Sheets - nobody else can see your data
  • No Backend Server: All authentication happens directly between you and Google
  • Revoke Access: You can revoke app access anytime from your Google Account settings
  • Local Storage: Some settings are cached in your browser for convenience

❓ Troubleshooting

  • Data Not Saving: Check your internet connection and try clicking "Save to Google Sheets" manually
  • Sheet Not Loading: Your sheet may have been deleted - click "Create New Sheet" in Settings
  • Sign In Issues: Clear your browser cache and cookies, then try signing in again
  • Charts Not Showing: Click "Refresh" in the Statistics tab to reload the charts

🔗 Useful Links

  • View Your Google Sheet: Go to Settings to find your Sheet ID, then visit https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/YOUR_SHEET_ID
  • Manage App Permissions: Visit Google Account Permissions
